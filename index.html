Meet Dr John Bacalakis

Dr John grew up in Cairns, graduated with Honours from the University of Queensland, and spent 16 years in private practice in Brisbane before opening River City Dental in Indooroopilly.

He has kept studying ever since. He graduated from the Kois Center in Seattle in 2016 and still attends its annual symposium, and he has trained extensively in implant dentistry, holding a Diplomate of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists and a Surgical Implant Fellowship with the Misch International Implant Institute.

With three kids of his own, he knows a dental visit should feel relaxed for every age. Outside the practice you'll find him on the sidelines at football and cricket, coaching or keeping score.

Offer terms

New patient appointment. Maximum out of pocket of $270 for adults and $130 for children under 15 without private health cover. Patients with private health cover pay the difference between $270 and their fund's rebate. Benefits paid above $270 are not refunded or credited. The appointment may include item numbers 011, 114 or 115, 121 and 022 (X-rays as required). Any additional treatment is outside the capped price and will be discussed and quoted before it goes ahead.

Custom mouthguard. Maximum out of pocket of $218. Patients with private health cover pay the gap, if any, between their fund's benefit and $218. Benefits paid above $218 are not refunded or credited.

Philips Zoom in-chair whitening. Maximum out of pocket of $803 including GST. Patients with private health cover pay the gap, if any, between their fund's benefit and $803. Benefits paid above $803 are not refunded or credited. Suitability is assessed at consultation.

Health funds. Annual limit reset dates vary by fund and policy. Check with your fund.

Dental warning. Any surgical or invasive procedure carries risks. Before proceeding, you should seek a second opinion from an appropriately qualified health practitioner. Treatment is only provided where it suits the individual patient, after examination and informed consent. Individual results vary.
